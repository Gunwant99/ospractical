Producer–Consumer Queue

In the exam system, bakers stand for students submitting answers, the shelf is the bounded submission queue, and customers are evaluation workers. A full queue blocks producers; an empty queue blocks consumers.

LIVETHREADS

Make the baker slow and customers wait. Make the customer slow and the shelf fills up so bakers wait. A lock lets only one thread touch the shelf at a time.
